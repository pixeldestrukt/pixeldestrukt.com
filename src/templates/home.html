01 Domains

  • Projection

    Mapping, warping and blending across irregular surfaces. Multi-output playback, edge blend, keystone correction, geometry that survives load-in.

    Pixel mapping at scale. sACN and Art-Net distribution, addressable strip and panel driving, power and data topology that does not brown out on a white frame.

  • Realtime graphics

    GLSL, compute passes, feedback systems. Custom generators and effect chains that run at frame rate on the hardware actually in the booth.

  • Embedded

    Microcontroller firmware for controllers, sensors and drivers. Timing-critical output, DMX and MIDI interfaces, devices that boot into showtime.
