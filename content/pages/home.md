Pixeldestrukt is the technical side of my practice: the **software, hardware and
signal plumbing** behind live visuals. Realtime graphics, LED pixel mapping,
projection systems and the embedded devices that drive them.

The performance work — sets, shows, residencies — lives at
[bl1t.com](https://bl1t.com). This is where the tools get built, documented and
occasionally taken apart in public.
