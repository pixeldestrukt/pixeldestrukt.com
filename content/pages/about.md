I design and build the technical layer of live visual work: the renderers, the
pixel maps, the controllers, the cabling diagrams, the firmware that has to come
up clean five minutes before doors.

Most of what I make falls into four buckets.

## Realtime graphics

Custom generators and effect chains in GLSL and C++, built to run at frame rate
on whatever machine is actually going to be in the booth — not on a workstation
with a spare 300 watts. Feedback systems, multi-pass compositing, ISF-compatible
shaders, and integration with the usual suspects (Resolume, TouchDesigner,
Syphon/Spout, NDI).

## LED systems

Pixel mapping from the fixture layout up: sACN/E1.31 and Art-Net universe
planning, addressable strip and panel driving, power injection topology, and the
unglamorous arithmetic of what happens when every pixel goes white at once.

## Projection

Warping, blending and mapping onto surfaces that were not designed to be
projected on. Multi-output playback, edge blend calibration, geometry that
survives being bumped during load-in.

## Embedded

Firmware for controllers, sensors and drivers — ESP32, RP2040, Teensy, bare
STM32 — with the timing discipline that DMX and addressable LED protocols
demand. Devices that boot into showtime rather than into a setup wizard.

## Working together

I take on technical direction, custom tool development, and system design for
installations and touring productions. If you have a surface, a signal chain or
a control problem that does not fit off-the-shelf gear, that is the interesting
case.
